# Laptop power and battery

## Battery charging failure
If the portable computer's battery percentage stays the same while plugged in, connect the supplied AC adapter directly to a working wall outlet. Disconnect any dock and check whether the charging indicator appears. If the battery still does not recharge, contact technical support with the device serial number.

## Unexpected shutdowns
If the computer powers off under load, clear the air vents and place the device on a hard surface. Record when the shutdown happens and include the details when contacting technical support.
